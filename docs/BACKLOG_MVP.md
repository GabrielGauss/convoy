# CONVOY — Backlog: MVP "One track, three vehicles" (DRAFT)

> **Status: draft for team review (2026-10-12).** Sources: D-035 (MVP), D-036 (track tech), D-033 (art), O-004 (falling between layers).
> Every phase ends in a playtest gate. If a gate fails, iterate that phase; do not start the next one.

**MVP goal:** crews of 1–3 race one ~10 km multi-level sprint to the Monolith, in a sidecar motorbike, the JACKAL or the heavy, each with its own lines, against one AI rival per class, with mounted weapons, a pre-race loadout and in-race upgrades.

**MVP exit questions:** Is the layered track the moat (players talk about lines, layers and jumps after a run)? Does each class have places where it shines? Do competent runs of each class finish within ±5%? Is falling between layers fun or frustrating? Do weapons create racing opportunities at top speed? Does upgrading change how you drive the rest of the run?

Owners: **GV** = Gabriel · **DEV** = Lead Dev · **ND** = Narrative & Game Designer · **AI** = Claude drafts, human owner reviews. Estimates are rough ideal hours; re-estimate after Phase 1.

## Phase 1 — Segment 1: three layers, JACKAL only (part of M1)

### TK-00 Track kit & spline spike · DEV (+AI) · ~10 h · P0
- Confirm D-036: Unity Splines road extrusion, bridge/ramp/ledge pieces, re-runnable generator script, AI racing line from the same spline. Ends with D-036 Accepted or amended.
- **AC:** one 300 m test road with a bridge over a lower road and one speed-gated ramp, generated by script; JACKAL drives it with no wheel snagging at seams.

### TK-01 Segment 1 (~2.5 km, three layers) · ND (layout) + GV (feel) (+AI) · ~24 h · P0
- High / mid / low layers crossing over and under each other; at least 2 branches, 2 shortcuts, 1 impossible jump; starts the route towards the Monolith.
- **O-004 toggle:** falling from a layer either lands on the lower route or resets, switchable in a config, so playtests decide.
- Keep the route within ~5 km of the world origin.
- **AC:** a first-time player completes it; each layer is used in ≥ 1 of 3 runs; both falling modes playable.
- **Deps:** TK-00, V-02 (recovery provider).

### PT-A Segment playtest · All · 2 h · P0 — **GATE**
- **Pass:** all three want to replay it, and O-004 gets an answer.

## Phase 2 — Three classes and their lines

### VH-01 Sidecar motorbike · GV (+AI) · ~16 h · P0
- Three-wheel raycast rig on the existing controller (D-004) with its own `VehicleConfig`; visual lean in presentation only; 2 seats (Pilot + sidecar Gunner).
- **AC:** best acceleration and turning of the three, weakest to impacts and mud; no new physics model; EditMode tests for any new math.

### VH-02 Heavy (H-02 MULE) · GV (+AI) · ~10 h · P0
- Own `VehicleConfig`; breaks Rammer walls (LV-01); reduced mud penalty (LV-02); can push lighter vehicles.
- **AC:** breaks a Rammer wall that the JACKAL cannot; slowest acceleration; worst jumper.

### CL-01 Class lines in segment 1 · ND + GV · ~16 h · P0
- At least one line per class per segment: narrow ledges and pipes (sidecar), speed-gated jumps (JACKAL), ram walls and brute lines (heavy). Prototype one fragile bridge the heavy collapses.
- **AC:** each class has a line where it gains time; competent runs of the three classes finish segment 1 within ±5%.
- **Deps:** VH-01, VH-02, TK-01.

### AI-03 One AI rival per class · DEV or GV (+AI) · ~12 h · P0
- Each rival follows its class lines from the spline data (D-019); same commands as players, no cheating physics.
- **AC:** each AI finishes segment 1 in ≥ 90% of runs and uses its class line.
- **Deps:** CL-01, A-01.

### PT-B Class playtest · All · 2 h · P0 — **GATE**
- **Pass:** each player can name a place where their class shone and one where it suffered.

## Phase 3 — Weapons, loadout, upgrades

### WP-01 Mounted weapons per class · GV (+AI) · ~16 h · P0
- 2–3 weapons from a fixed pool (e.g. autocannon, rear mines, ram spikes); ammo and heat; no item boxes. Builds on G-01.
- **AC:** a hit at top speed creates an overtake or a route change in ≥ 1 observed instance per session.

### LO-01 Pre-race loadout · AI → GV review · ~8 h · P0
- Pick weapons and one module from a fixed pool per class; capacity limits force trade-offs (pillar 2). No persistence.
- **AC:** two players with the same class pick different loadouts in playtest.

### UPG-01 Scrap & workshop upgrades · GV (+AI) · ~12 h · P0
- Scrap on risky lines; workshop between segments spends it on upgrades (armour, cooling, nitro, ammo). Upgrades last the run only.
- **AC:** playtesters can name one upgrade that changed how they drove afterwards.

### PT-C Combat & upgrade playtest · All · 2 h · P0 — **GATE**
