Measured: 0–100 km/h **3.4 s**; 6 s full throttle **147 km/h** without nitro, **177 km/h** with 3 s of nitro; full-lock turn at 147 km/h stays flat (no tipping, 4/4 wheels down).

Drift test (entry 80 km/h, full lock + handbrake 1.2 s, then counter-steer): mult 0.35 → 4° max slip; 0.20 → 10°; 0.10 → 28° but bleeds to 45 km/h; **0.12 + brake 1200 → 20°, keeps 57 km/h** (chosen); 0.15 + 1000 → 14°. Vehicle stayed upright in every run.
